Dr. Young Suk Bang, Managing Director of FNC Technology Co., Ltd.’s U.S. Maryland Branch, gave a talk titled “Analysis of Global Nuclear Industry Trends and Business Opportunities,” drawing on his more than 20 years of experience in the nuclear industry in South Korea. The seminar was held at the Consulate General of the Republic of Korea in Boston, followed by a discussion on the key challenges to strengthening U.S.-Korea cooperation in the nuclear industry, as well as opportunities for investment and business expansion by Korean nuclear companies in the United States.
